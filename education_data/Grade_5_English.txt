LESSON 1: Voice
EXPLANATION: In active voice the subject performs an action. In passive voice it receives the action.
QUESTION: Who performs the action in active voice?
ANSWER: The subject.

LESSON 2: Reported Speech
EXPLANATION: Direct speech gives exact spoken words. Indirect speech reports their meaning.
QUESTION: What is direct speech?
ANSWER: Exact spoken words.

LESSON 3: Summary
EXPLANATION: A summary gives the main ideas of a text in fewer words without adding unsupported details.
QUESTION: What belongs in a summary?
ANSWER: Main ideas.
