LESSON 1: Plant Parts
EXPLANATION: Roots anchor a plant and absorb water. Stems support leaves. Leaves use sunlight to make food.
QUESTION: What do roots do?
ANSWER: Anchor plants and absorb water.

LESSON 2: Food
EXPLANATION: Different foods supply energy and nutrients. Eat a variety of fruits, vegetables, grains and protein sources.
QUESTION: Why eat varied food?
ANSWER: To get a variety of nutrients.

LESSON 3: Environment
EXPLANATION: Living and nonliving things make up our environment. Air and water are nonliving things.
QUESTION: Name two nonliving things.
ANSWER: Air and water.
