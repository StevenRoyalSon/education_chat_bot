LESSON 1: Solar System
EXPLANATION: The Sun is a star. Earth is a planet orbiting the Sun. The Moon is Earths natural satellite.
QUESTION: Is the Sun a star?
ANSWER: Yes.

LESSON 2: Food Chain
EXPLANATION: Grass can be eaten by a grasshopper, which can be eaten by a frog.
QUESTION: What is the producer?
ANSWER: Grass.

LESSON 3: Resources
EXPLANATION: Water, air, soil and forests are natural resources. Conserving them helps ecosystems.
QUESTION: Name two natural resources.
ANSWER: Water and soil.
