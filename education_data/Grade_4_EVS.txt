LESSON 1: Water Cycle
EXPLANATION: Water evaporates when heated, condenses into clouds and returns as precipitation.
QUESTION: What is evaporation?
ANSWER: Liquid water changing to water vapour.

LESSON 2: Adaptation
EXPLANATION: Animals have features suited to habitats. Fish use gills to obtain oxygen from water.
QUESTION: Why do fish have gills?
ANSWER: To obtain oxygen from water.

LESSON 3: Waste
EXPLANATION: Reduce means use less. Reuse means use again. Recycle means process material for another use.
QUESTION: What does reuse mean?
ANSWER: Use again.
