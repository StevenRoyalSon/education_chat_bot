LESSON 1: Verbs
EXPLANATION: A verb expresses an action or state. Jump, read and is are examples.
QUESTION: What is a verb?
ANSWER: A word expressing action or state.

LESSON 2: Paragraphs
EXPLANATION: A paragraph is a group of related sentences about one main idea.
QUESTION: What is a paragraph about?
ANSWER: One main idea.

LESSON 3: Punctuation
EXPLANATION: Use a question mark at the end of a direct question.
QUESTION: Which mark ends a question?
ANSWER: A question mark.
