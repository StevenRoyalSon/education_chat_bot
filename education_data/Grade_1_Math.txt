LESSON 1: Counting
EXPLANATION: Counting tells how many objects are in a group. Four apples means a group of four.
QUESTION: How many apples are in a group of four?
ANSWER: Four.

LESSON 2: Addition
EXPLANATION: Adding combines groups. Two pencils plus three pencils equals five pencils.
QUESTION: What is 2 plus 3?
ANSWER: 5.

LESSON 3: Shapes
EXPLANATION: A triangle has three sides. A square has four equal sides. A circle has no straight sides.
QUESTION: How many sides does a triangle have?
ANSWER: Three.
