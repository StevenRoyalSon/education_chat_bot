LESSON 1: Factors
EXPLANATION: A factor divides a number without remainder. Three is a factor of twelve.
QUESTION: Is 3 a factor of 12?
ANSWER: Yes.

LESSON 2: Equivalent Fractions
EXPLANATION: One half and two quarters are equivalent fractions.
QUESTION: Is 1/2 equal to 2/4?
ANSWER: Yes.

LESSON 3: Area
EXPLANATION: Area measures space inside a flat shape. A 6 cm by 4 cm rectangle has area 24 square centimetres.
QUESTION: What is the area of a 6 by 4 rectangle?
ANSWER: 24 square centimetres.
