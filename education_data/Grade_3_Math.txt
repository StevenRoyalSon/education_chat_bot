LESSON 1: Fractions
EXPLANATION: A fraction shows equal parts of a whole. One half means one of two equal parts.
QUESTION: What does one half mean?
ANSWER: One of two equal parts.

LESSON 2: Division
EXPLANATION: Division can share things equally. Twelve sweets shared by three children gives four each.
QUESTION: What is 12 divided by 3?
ANSWER: 4.

LESSON 3: Perimeter
EXPLANATION: Perimeter is distance around a shape. A 5 cm by 3 cm rectangle has perimeter 16 cm.
QUESTION: What is the perimeter of a 5 by 3 rectangle?
ANSWER: 16 cm.
