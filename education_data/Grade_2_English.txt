LESSON 1: Pronouns
EXPLANATION: Pronouns replace nouns. He, she, it, we and they are pronouns.
QUESTION: Name one pronoun.
ANSWER: She.

LESSON 2: Adjectives
EXPLANATION: An adjective describes a noun. Red describes the ball in red ball.
QUESTION: What is the adjective in red ball?
ANSWER: Red.

LESSON 3: Past Tense
EXPLANATION: Past tense describes something that happened earlier. Walk becomes walked.
QUESTION: What is the past tense of walk?
ANSWER: Walked.
