LESSON 1: Our Senses
EXPLANATION: We see with eyes, hear with ears, smell with a nose and taste with a tongue.
QUESTION: What helps us hear?
ANSWER: Ears.

LESSON 2: Healthy Habits
EXPLANATION: Wash hands with soap before eating and after using the toilet. Drink safe water.
QUESTION: When should you wash hands?
ANSWER: Before eating and after using the toilet.

LESSON 3: Plants
EXPLANATION: Plants need water, air and light. Roots absorb water from soil.
QUESTION: What absorbs water?
ANSWER: Roots.
