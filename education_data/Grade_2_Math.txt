LESSON 1: Place Value
EXPLANATION: In 42, the 4 represents four tens and the 2 represents two ones.
QUESTION: What does 4 represent in 42?
ANSWER: 40.

LESSON 2: Multiplication
EXPLANATION: Multiplication can count equal groups. Three groups of four equal twelve.
QUESTION: What is 3 times 4?
ANSWER: 12.

LESSON 3: Length
EXPLANATION: One metre is equal to one hundred centimetres.
QUESTION: How many centimetres in a metre?
ANSWER: 100.
