LESSON 1: Water
EXPLANATION: We use water for drinking, cooking and cleaning. Avoid wasting water.
QUESTION: Name two uses of water.
ANSWER: Drinking and cooking.

LESSON 2: Shelter
EXPLANATION: Houses provide shelter from weather. Different climates may need different houses.
QUESTION: Why do we need houses?
ANSWER: For shelter.

LESSON 3: Helpers
EXPLANATION: Farmers grow food, teachers help us learn and doctors care for sick people.
QUESTION: Who grows food?
ANSWER: Farmers.
