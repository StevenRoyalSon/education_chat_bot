LESSON 1: Nouns
EXPLANATION: A noun names a person, place, animal, or thing. Teacher, school, cat and book are nouns.
QUESTION: Is school a noun?
ANSWER: Yes.

LESSON 2: Verbs
EXPLANATION: Verbs describe actions or states. Run, read and jump are action words.
QUESTION: Give an action word.
ANSWER: Run.

LESSON 3: Sentences
EXPLANATION: A sentence communicates a complete thought. It begins with a capital letter.
QUESTION: How does a sentence begin?
ANSWER: With a capital letter.
