LESSON 1: Decimals
EXPLANATION: In 3.25, 3 is the whole number, 2 is tenths and 5 is hundredths.
QUESTION: What place is 5 in 3.25?
ANSWER: Hundredths.

LESSON 2: Percent
EXPLANATION: Percent means out of one hundred. 25 percent equals one quarter.
QUESTION: What fraction equals 25 percent?
ANSWER: One quarter.

LESSON 3: Volume
EXPLANATION: Volume measures three-dimensional space. A 2 by 3 by 4 cm box has volume 24 cubic centimetres.
QUESTION: What is the volume of the box?
ANSWER: 24 cubic centimetres.
