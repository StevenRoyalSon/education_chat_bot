LESSON 1: Subject
EXPLANATION: In The birds sing, The birds is the subject and sing is the predicate.
QUESTION: What is the subject?
ANSWER: The birds.

LESSON 2: Antonyms
EXPLANATION: Antonyms are words with opposite meanings, like hot and cold.
QUESTION: What is the opposite of hot?
ANSWER: Cold.

LESSON 3: Comprehension
EXPLANATION: Reading comprehension involves finding main ideas and details in a passage.
QUESTION: What supports an answer?
ANSWER: Details from the passage.
